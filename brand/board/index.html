01 — The mark

An A-frame with a Z inside it

The outer form is a roofline — the shape of a house under construction, and the letter A. Locked inside it is a Z, for Zavala. The Z's diagonal is the only stroke that carries brass, so the monogram still reads A‑Z at favicon size.

03 — Clearspace & minimum size

Give it room

Keep clear space equal to one third of the mark's height on every side. Nothing — type, edges, photography — enters that zone. Minimum sizes below are where the Z is still legible; go smaller and use the badge instead.

04 — Colour

Navy, cream, brass

Navy carries the brand; cream is the voice; brass is the accent and never more than a few percent of any layout. Ink and slate are supporting neutrals.

06 — Usage

Do and don't

✓ Do

Use the supplied files. Put the primary mark on navy or cream. Keep the ⅓ clearspace. Use the badge when the space is small or square.

✕ Don't

Don't stretch, rotate, or re-draw the mark. Don't change the stroke weight.

✓ Do

Reverse to one-colour white or black when a vendor needs a single ink — embroidery, etched glass, stamps.

✕ Don't

Don't recolour the mark outside the palette, and don't put brass on brass or navy on ink — there isn't enough contrast.

✓ Do

Place the mark over the calm part of a photograph, and darken the image behind it if needed.

✕ Don't

Don't add drop shadows, outlines, gradients or bevels to the mark. It is a line drawing; keep it flat.
