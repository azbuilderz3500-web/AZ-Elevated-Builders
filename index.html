® AZ Elevated Builders — Brentwood & the East Bay, California

AZ Elevated Builders remodels and builds homes across the East Bay — from Brentwood and Discovery Bay to Walnut Creek, Oakland and Napa. Interiors, exteriors and concrete, carried by one crew from first walkthrough to final sign-off.

Every trade on our crew has spent fifteen-plus years in that one trade — the drywall, the paint, the finish carpentry. Estimates are free, the schedule is honest, and the work carries a written warranty of up to five years.

We are a family business built on one thing: work you will never have to redo. High-end finishes, straight lines, clean job sites, and one signature on the job from demo day to the final walkthrough.

Our
Services

Design & Planning

Bring finished plans or just an idea — we work with trusted designers when a project calls for one, and every job starts with a free on-site estimate, a clear scope and an honest schedule.

Construction

Kitchens, bathrooms, whole-home remodels, additions and ADUs, concrete and driveways, painting, flooring, drywall, cabinetry and finish carpentry — inside and out, all under one licensed crew.

  • The room that earns its keep — in daily use and at resale. We rework layout, lighting, cabinetry, stone and fixtures into a kitchen that feels inevitable rather than assembled, and we keep plumbing and electrical in-house so nothing waits on a sub. See Kitchen Remodeling across the East Bay →

  • Quiet, tactile rooms — tile, stone and calm light. Steam showers, radiant heated floors and saunas are the add-ons our clients ask for most, and we build them in from the rough-in, not as an afterthought. See Bathroom Remodeling across the East Bay →

  • Interior and exterior, top to bottom — circulation, light, finishes and systems reworked as one project. Plumbing, electrical, drywall and paint stay under our roof, which matters in older East Bay housing stock where surprises behind the walls are the rule. See Whole-Home Remodeling across the East Bay →

  • New square footage that reads as if it was always there — and accessory dwelling units that stand on their own as rental income or family space. We handle feasibility, California's ADU rules and local permitting through construction. See Additions & ADUs across the East Bay →

  • Driveways, patios, walkways and retaining walls — formed, poured and finished by our own crew. Quick turnarounds without cutting the prep that keeps concrete from cracking two winters in. See Concrete & Driveways across the East Bay →

  • Our painter has 22 years on the brush. Full prep, clean masking, quality coatings and straight cut lines — inside, outside, cabinets and trim. See Interior & Exterior Painting across the East Bay →

  • Hardwood, engineered hardwood, tile and laminate — plus radiant heated flooring where you want warmth underfoot. Level substrates, tight seams, clean transitions. See Flooring across the East Bay →

  • Hung, taped, textured and finished by a specialist with two decades in the trade. Walls you'll never think about again — which is the point. See Drywall across the East Bay →

  • Custom and semi-custom cabinetry, stone and quartz counters — specified, templated and installed as one scope so the reveal lines actually line up. See Cabinetry & Countertops across the East Bay →

  • Baseboards, casing, wainscot, built-ins and stair work — the details that separate a remodel from a renovation. Measured twice, scribed to the wall, filled and painted. See Finish Carpentry & Trim across the East Bay →

  • The upgrades clients ask for by name. Waterproofing done right, generators sized right, controls where you actually want them — spa features that work for decades. See Steam Showers, Saunas & Heated Floors across the East Bay →

  • Pergolas, landscaping, hardscape and the outdoor rooms East Bay weather was made for — planned as part of the house, not a follow-up phase. See Pergolas & Outdoor Living across the East Bay →
