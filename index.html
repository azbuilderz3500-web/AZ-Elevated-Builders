Frequently asked questions.

What areas does AZ Elevated Builders serve?

AZ Elevated Builders is based in Brentwood, California and serves a 60-mile radius across the East Bay, Tri-Valley, Delta, Solano and Napa counties — including Antioch, Oakley, Discovery Bay, Concord, Walnut Creek, Danville, San Ramon, Livermore, Tracy, Oakland, Berkeley and Napa.

Does AZ Elevated Builders offer free estimates?

Yes. Every project starts with a free on-site estimate that includes a written scope and a proposed schedule. Financing options are also available.

Is AZ Elevated Builders licensed?

Yes. AZ Elevated Builders is a licensed California general contractor (CSLB #1106795) based in Brentwood, and most of the crew have more than 15 years of experience in their trade.

What warranty does AZ Elevated Builders provide?

All work carries a written warranty of up to five years.

Does AZ Elevated Builders speak Spanish?

Yes — the team speaks both English and Spanish. Hablamos español.
