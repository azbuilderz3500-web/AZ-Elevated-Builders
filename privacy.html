Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 11, 2026

AZ Elevated Builders ("we," "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Information we collect

When you request a quote or assessment — on our website or through our ads (including Facebook/Instagram lead forms) — we collect the information you provide: your name, phone number, email address, property city, and details about your project.

Website and campaign information

When you submit an estimate request, we also receive the page you submitted from and campaign information present in its link, such as UTM parameters and Google click identifiers. We use this information to understand which inquiries come from our marketing. Requests are processed through Make, our form integration provider. We do not send your name, phone number or project details to an analytics service through the website’s interaction events.

The website does not currently load advertising or analytics cookies. If advertising measurement is enabled later, this policy and the applicable privacy controls will be updated before it is used.

How we use it

We use your contact and project information to respond to your request: scheduling assessments, preparing quotes, and communicating about your project. We do not sell your personal information.

Sharing

We share information only with service providers that help us operate (for example, scheduling and communication tools), and only as needed to serve you.

Data retention & your choices

We keep lead information only as long as needed for business purposes. You may request access to or deletion of your information at any time by contacting us.
