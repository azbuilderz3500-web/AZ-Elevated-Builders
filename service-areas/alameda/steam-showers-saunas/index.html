Frequently asked questions

Can a steam shower go in my existing bathroom?

In most cases yes — the enclosure needs to be sealed and sized to the generator, and we confirm both at the free estimate. Retrofit is very doable when it's planned from the rough-in rather than bolted on.

Are heated floors expensive to run?

Less than most people expect — modern systems zone by room, run on programmable thermostats, and warm the tile you touch rather than the whole house. Bathrooms typically cost pennies a day to run.

Do you install saunas in Alameda?

Yes — indoor conversions and outdoor builds both. Alameda homeowners usually pair a sauna with a bath remodel or a backyard project, and we design it into the larger scope.
