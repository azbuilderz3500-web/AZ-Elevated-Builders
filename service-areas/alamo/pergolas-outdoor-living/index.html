Do pergolas need permits in Alamo?

Whether a pergola needs a permit in Alamo depends on its size, its height and whether it attaches to the house. Each city sets its own rules, and many exempt small detached structures. We check your city's rules for your design and pull the permit when one is needed.

Wood or aluminum pergola?

Wood offers a natural finish and flexible detailing; aluminum generally needs less maintenance. We build both and can help you compare appearance, upkeep and site exposure.
