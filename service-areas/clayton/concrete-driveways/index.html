Frequently asked questions

How long before I can use my new driveway?

Foot traffic in 24–48 hours, vehicles in about 7 days as the slab cures toward full strength. We'll give you exact numbers for your pour and weather.

How fast can you pour a driveway in Clayton?

Driveways are one of our quickest turnarounds — most tear-out-and-repour projects in Clayton run 3–5 working days from demo to finished pour, weather permitting.

Do you build retaining walls?

Yes — engineered where height requires it, with proper drainage behind the wall. Failed retaining walls are almost always drainage failures, so we treat the part you can't see as the most important part.
