Frequently asked questions

Custom or semi-custom — which should I choose?

Semi-custom covers most kitchens beautifully at a better price point; full custom earns its cost with unusual spaces, specific woods or furniture-grade details. We'll show you both against your budget at the estimate.

How long do new counters take?

We template after cabinets are set, fabrication runs 1–2 weeks, and installation is usually a day. We sequence plumbing reconnection the same week so you're not without a sink.

Do you install cabinetry outside of full remodels in Mountain House?

Yes — cabinet and counter replacement is a common standalone project for Mountain House homeowners who want the biggest visual upgrade without a full gut.
