Frequently asked questions

Can you match my existing wall texture?

Yes — texture matching is the difference between a repair and an eyesore, and it's a specialty of ours. Orange peel, knockdown, hand troweled or smooth, the patch should disappear after paint.

Do you remove popcorn ceilings in Oakland?

Yes, including testing considerations for older Oakland homes. We scrape, skim and refinish to a modern smooth or light texture, and leave the room paint-ready.

How fast can a repair be done?

Small patches are often same-day with a return visit for texture and touch-up after drying. Larger repairs run 2–4 days because mud needs to dry between coats — rushing it is how bad patches happen.
