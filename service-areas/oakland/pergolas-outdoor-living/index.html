Frequently asked questions

Do pergolas need permits in Oakland?

It depends on size, height and attachment to the house — Oakland exempts some smaller detached structures. We confirm requirements for your design and pull the permit when one's needed.

Wood or aluminum pergola?

Wood wins on warmth and customization; aluminum wins on maintenance. We build both and will talk honestly about how each ages in Delta sun before you choose.

Can you do the whole backyard — concrete, pergola and landscaping?

That's exactly how we prefer to build it: one plan, one schedule, one crew, so grades, drainage, footings and planting work together instead of being three contractors' problems.
