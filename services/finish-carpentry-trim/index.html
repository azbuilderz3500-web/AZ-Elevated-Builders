Finish Carpentry & Trim
East Bay & Beyond

Baseboards, casing, wainscot, built-ins and stairs — the details that separate remodels from renovations. Based in Brentwood and serving a 60-mile radius across Contra Costa, Alameda, San Joaquin, Solano and Napa counties.

Trim is the jewelry of the house — and the first place cheap work shows. Our finish carpenters measure twice, scribe to the wall, and fill and caulk so paint looks poured on. Baseboards, door and window casing, crown, wainscot and paneling, floating shelves, built-ins and stair work.

It's also the highest-return finishing touch on a repaint or flooring project: new floors deserve new base, and fresh walls deserve crisp casing.

What's included

  • Baseboards & door/window casing
  • Crown molding
  • Wainscot, board-and-batten & paneling
  • Custom built-ins & floating shelves
  • Stair rails, treads & skirt boards
  • Interior door replacement & hanging
  • Mantels & feature walls
  • Paint-grade & stain-grade work
